Cherry OS

A friendly desktop for your PC, built on Debian 13. Try it straight from a USB stick, then install it when you're ready.

Download Cherry OS 1.0 How to install

One 4.7 GB ISO for 64-bit PCs. Flash it to a USB stick of 8 GB or more with balenaEtcher or Rufus. Checksums

What's inside

  • Windows apps just work. Double-click a .exe or .msi and it runs through Wine, which comes ready with .NET support. Installed programs appear in your app menu.
  • The Cherry Store. Apps published by anyone, with no review queue. Every app is signed by its publisher and virus-scanned, and warnings are shown before you install. Browse the store.
  • Five desktops: KDE Plasma, GNOME, Cinnamon, Xfce and Budgie. Try each one live, pick one in the installer, and switch any time in Cherry Center.
  • Programming languages built in: Python 3 (with Thonny), Java, Node.js, C and C++, Rust, Go, Ruby, PHP, Lua, Perl and SQLite.
  • Bring your files along with Cherry Move. Plug in the USB stick from your old computer, even in the installer, and your files come across.
  • Updates until 30 June 2030. Security fixes install by themselves every day, and nothing restarts your computer without asking.
