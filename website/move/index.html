Cherry Move

Bring your files from your old Windows, Mac or Linux computer to Cherry OS, encrypted the whole way.

Windows macOS Linux (.deb)

Also on every Cherry OS USB stick. More downloads: .rpm, ARM and standalone Linux.

How it works

  • On your old computer, pick what to bring. Cherry Move compresses your files, stores identical files only once, and encrypts everything before it leaves.
  • Keep them where suits you: on one or more USB sticks with no internet needed, in your own Google Drive, OneDrive or MEGA, or on a NAS or another computer at home.
  • On Cherry OS, plug in the USB stick and your files are found by themselves, even in the installer. Or type the 34-character code you saved: print it, keep it, or scan its QR code with your phone.
  • Private by design. Nothing ever leaves unencrypted, and sign-ins to your cloud drive or NAS are forgotten when Cherry Move closes. Files kept in your cloud drive or NAS can't be read without your code.

Keep your USB stick as safe as your files. So that your files restore by themselves, the code that unlocks them is kept on the stick too. Anyone who has the stick can get your files back, including saved passwords if you brought your browsers or apps.

Don't lose it or lend it out, and once your files are on Cherry OS, delete the CHERRY-TRANSFER folder or reformat the stick. If that's not good enough for you, keep your files in your own cloud drive or NAS instead. Only the code goes on the stick then, and the files can't be read without signing in to your storage too.

Source code

Cherry Move's source is in the Cherry OS repository (move/ for Windows, macOS and other Linux, desktop/ for the Cherry OS app).
